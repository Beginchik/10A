A room with a view: Loch Ness

A hotel room in Scotland. What can you see in the room? What can you see outside the window?

In this lesson, you will talk about hotel rooms and hotels with a view over Loch Ness, read tourist information about Scotland, and learn interesting facts and rumours about the lake. You will also learn how to use there is / there are with a, an, some, any, and prepositions of place (in, on, under, …).

Speaking

At the reception: a room with a view

Role-play with your teacher

You arrive at the Loch Ness View Hotel. Act out the dialogue, then change places.

You = guest. Ask about: a room with a view · the bathroom (shower or bath?) · towels · the restaurant and bar · the gym and the pool. Ask 4–5 questions with Is there …? / Are there any …?
Teacher = receptionist. Answer with short answers and add one problem: e.g. “No, there aren’t any towels today, sorry!” or “There isn’t a lift, but there are stairs!”

Useful language

  • Hello! Is there a room with a view, please?
  • Are there any towels in the room?
  • Yes, there is. There’s a lovely view of the lake.
  • There’s a restaurant on the ground floor.
  • Oh no! / Great! Thank you very much.
